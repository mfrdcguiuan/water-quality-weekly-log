# Literature behind the weekly interpretation

The weekly interpretation compares your readings with ranges reported for blue swimming crab (*Portunus pelagicus*)
hatchery rearing. It is a summary of the studies below, mostly read from their published abstracts and excerpts, not a
systematic review of every paper on the species. Please check the original papers before relying on any single number.

## How the app turns your readings into the text
- Each treatment's weekly mean is compared with fixed ranges: temperature 28-30 C (below 26 C, 26-28 C, 30.5-31.5 C and 31.5 C or above are called out), salinity 30-35 ppt, pH 8.0-8.2 (7.8-8.4 normal seawater), dissolved oxygen 5 mg/L or more (4-6 mg/L reported as needed).
- The ranges are set by the literature above, not by the Target ranges you can edit in the app.
- Evidence is thin for some cases, for example 32 C and above in larvae and salinity above 35 ppt; the text says so rather than claiming certainty.
- Differences between treatments are described, but no statistical test is run.
